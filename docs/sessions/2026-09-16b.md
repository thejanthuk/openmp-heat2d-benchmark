## Measured today

**The theory is quantitatively exact.** Seeding the checkerboard (the worst mode) and comparing the
centre value to `G^n` where `G = 1 - 4*(rx+ry)`:

| `rx+ry` | `G` | 20 steps predicted | measured |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.55 | -1.2 | 38.3376 | **38.3376** |
| 0.40 | -0.6 | 3.65616e-5 | **3.65611e-5** |

Six significant figures including sign. Also: with a *smooth* initial condition the same unstable
run looked fine for 150 steps and only exploded past 250 — because the smooth mode carries no energy
at the unstable wavenumber and the instability had to grow from round-off (~1e-16 at `|G|=1.2` needs
`16/log10(1.2) = 202` steps; observed between 150 and 250).

**Lesson: a short test run of an unstable scheme can look completely correct.**
